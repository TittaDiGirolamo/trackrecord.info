## 1. Purpose

This Editorial Charter defines the non-negotiable standards that govern how predictions are logged, reviewed, resolved, and published on Trackrecord.info.  

It exists to protect the integrity of the public record and to make the project’s quality system transparent and auditable.

---

## 2. Core Commitments

Trackrecord.info operates under the following commitments:

1. **Verifiability** — Every logged prediction and every resolution must be traceable to publicly available evidence.
2. **Independence** — Judgments are made without regard to the forecaster’s identity, reputation, popularity, or past performance.
3. **Fidelity** — The original statement is never silently reinterpreted or softened to produce a more favourable score.
4. **Transparency** — The reasoning behind every resolution is recorded and available for public inspection.
5. **No retroactive adjustment** — Once a prediction is resolved, its outcome is permanent except for the correction of clear factual errors in the source material.

---

## 3. Quality Gates

Two mandatory checklists serve as the operational quality gates:

| Gate | Document | When applied |
|------|----------|--------------|
| Logging | [LOGGING_CHECKLIST.md](LOGGING_CHECKLIST.md) v1.2 | Before any prediction is promoted into the live dataset |
| Resolution | [RESOLUTION_CHECKLIST.md](RESOLUTION_CHECKLIST.md) v1.2 | Before any prediction is marked resolved |

Nothing enters or leaves the live dataset without passing the relevant checklist. Human review remains mandatory in both cases.

---

## 4. Independence

Reviewers must declare the absence of any material conflict of interest with the forecaster or the subject matter.  

A material conflict exists when there is a reasonable risk that personal, financial, professional, or reputational interests could influence the judgment.  

The required process is:
- Explicit self-declaration on every review
- Light public-information check
- Escalation when red flags or high-stakes claims appear

Full details are recorded in the Independence & Process Integrity dimension of both checklists.

---

## 5. Special Scoring Rules

### Compound and conjunctive predictions
Conjunctive claims (“A and B”) are scored all-or-nothing. The prediction is correct only if every major sub-claim is true.  

### Exact-score and highly specific predictions
These are scored strictly according to the stated criteria. No charitable or “close enough” interpretation is permitted.

### Soft or hedged language
When the original statement is clearly hedged or caveated, the resolution criteria must remain faithful to that language. Converting soft language into a firm binary claim requires explicit documentation and is recorded as a Minor under the Resolution Checklist.

Sample audits using RESOLUTION_CHECKLIST.md will be conducted periodically and published.

---

## 7. Change Control

This Charter, the two checklists, and METHODOLOGY.md are living documents.  

Material changes require:
- An explicit version increment
- A short public note explaining the reason for the change
- Preservation of prior versions for auditability

Minor clarifications may be made without a full version bump provided the change is recorded.

---

## 8. Scope

This Charter applies to all predictions logged and resolved under the current WC2026 English-language focus and to any future domains that adopt the same methodology.
