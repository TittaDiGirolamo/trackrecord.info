How it works

Transparent process

1
Prediction is logged
A public prediction is recorded with its source, timestamp and resolution criteria. It enters the dataset as pending.
2
Human review
A human checks that the prediction is unambiguous, has a clear time horizon and can be resolved against primary evidence.
3
Published
The prediction becomes part of the permanent public record and is visible on the site while it awaits resolution.
1
Actual result
When the time horizon is reached, the real-world outcome is established from primary or official sources.
2
Human review
A human verifies the outcome against the original resolution criteria and records the rationale.
3
Published
The resolved prediction and its score become permanent public record. Forecaster metrics update automatically.

Full details in METHODOLOGY.md. Everything is designed for transparency and repeatability.

The mission

Most public predictions disappear without accountability

Trackrecord.info aims to create a permanent, transparent record so that everyone can see who gets it right over time. Follow along for updates and new forecasters.

We’ll only email you when we have meaningful updates.
