# 90-Day Retrospective — Trackrecord.info

**Version:** 0.1

**Plan reference:** 90-Day Action Plan v1.0

---

## 1. Original Intent

The 90-day plan set out to prove the core loop:

**log → resolve (human-reviewed) → score → display**

with high verification integrity, using FIFA World Cup 2026 predictions from English-language sources, while keeping the feature set deliberately minimal.

Primary success criteria were:
- 50–70+ resolved predictions from ≥ 10–12 distinct figures
- Published methodology and Editorial Charter
- Early demand signals (originally framed as sign-ups)
- A clean public record

---

## 2. What Was Achieved

### Data and verification
- Resolved predictions: **73** (target 50–70+)
- Distinct resolved forecasters: **25** (target ≥ 10–12)
- Formal quality gates published and active:
  - LOGGING_CHECKLIST.md v1.2
  - RESOLUTION_CHECKLIST.md v1.2
- First sample audit under the Resolution Checklist: **20/20 Pass (100 %)**  
  Report: [audits/2026-08-17-resolution-checklist-sample-audit.md](audits/2026-08-17-resolution-checklist-sample-audit.md)

### Governance
- METHODOLOGY.md maintained and updated
- EDITORIAL_CHARTER.md v1.0 published
- Independence process defined and documented
- Stale partial-scoring references removed
- Formal scoring remains pure Brier (binary outcome)

### Public record & light outreach
- Core verification loop is operating under explicit, published standards
- Public note of completion published in the repository (UPDATES.md)
- Short factual update posted on X
- Light outreach sent to 6 people (3 external + 3 from personal network)

---

## 3. What Was Not Achieved

| Target | Result | Comment |
|--------|--------|---------|
| Sign-ups | 0 | Original demand proxy did not materialise |
| Significant organic traffic | Low | 53 unique visitors and 5 completed accountability lookups in the 28 days before the final surface attempt |

The absence of sign-ups is a clear gap relative to the original plan numbers. It does not invalidate the primary objective of proving a high-integrity verification system.

---

## 4. Signal Reflection

During the period it became clear that **completed accountability lookups** are a more mission-aligned early signal than email sign-ups.

A completed lookup (user selects a figure → views the track record → opens at least one resolved prediction with full evidence) directly measures whether the core value is being delivered. Sign-ups measure only intent to be contacted.

Going forward, completed accountability lookups will be treated as the primary early demand signal. Sign-ups remain secondary.

---

## 5. Overall Judgment

The original core objective — establishing a transparent, auditable, high-integrity system for logging and resolving public predictions — has been met and, on the quality dimensions, exceeded.

The demand-side targets were not met. This is recorded without rationalisation.

The project now has:
- Explicit quality gates
- A published Editorial Charter
- A completed public sample audit
- A clean separation between formal scoring and any legacy display fields

This constitutes a solid foundation for whatever comes next.

---

---

## 7. Closing Statement

The 90-day plan is closed on the dimension of verification integrity.  

The system works as designed. The documents are public. The first formal audit has been published. The absence of sign-ups is noted.

Further development can now proceed from a clear and honest baseline.
